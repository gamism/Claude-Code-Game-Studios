# Quality Gates

Before marking work complete, check:

- Product: the feature solves the stated player problem.
- UX: common flows are easy to complete on mobile and desktop.
- Frontend: loading, empty, error, and success states are implemented.
- Data: contracts, cache, and failure behavior are clear.
- SEO: page intent, metadata, headings, and links match the content.
- QA: important routes and states were tested.
- Performance: images, layout stability, and network behavior are reasonable.

If a gate is skipped, record why in `session-state/active.md`.
